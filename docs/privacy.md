# QR Lens Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 August 2026**

QR Lens is a free QR code scanning utility for spatial computing devices. This Privacy Policy explains how QR Lens handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Data Collection

QR Lens does not collect personal information.

QR Lens does not require an account and does not collect:

- Names or email addresses
- Meta account information
- Device identifiers
- Location information
- Usage analytics
- Advertising identifiers
- Browsing history
- Audio or voice recordings
- Controller or hand-tracking data for analytics or profiling

QR Lens uses QR-code detection capabilities provided by the headset platform to identify QR codes in the user's environment.

The decoded QR-code payload, such as a URL or text string, is processed locally by QR Lens solely to provide the scanning functionality.

QR Lens does not upload QR-code contents to a server.

QR Lens does not record, store, transmit, or share images of the user's physical environment.

## 2. Data Use

QR-code information is used only to provide the core functionality of QR Lens.

When a QR code is detected, QR Lens may:

- Display the decoded text or URL.
- Determine whether the QR code contains a valid HTTP or HTTPS web address.
- Allow the user to choose whether to open that address.

QR-code contents are not retained by QR Lens after they are no longer required for the current interaction.

QR Lens does not use scanned information for advertising, profiling, analytics, marketing, or any other secondary purpose.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

QR Lens does not sell, rent, transmit, or share personal information or QR-code contents with third parties.

If the user chooses **Open Link**, QR Lens asks the headset operating system to open the selected URL in the system web browser.

Once the user visits an external website, that website operates independently and may collect information according to its own privacy policy. QR Lens has no control over the privacy practices or content of external websites.

The headset operating system and platform services are governed by the respective platform provider's own terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Data Storage, Retention and Security

QR Lens does not operate a backend server and does not maintain a user database.

QR Lens does not maintain cloud storage of:

- QR-code scans
- QR-code contents
- User activity
- Personal information

Because QR Lens does not collect or retain personal information on its servers, there is no server-side personal information to request, export, or delete.

Any application data stored locally by the operating system can be removed by uninstalling QR Lens from the headset.

QR Lens is designed to minimise data handling and keep QR-code processing required by the application on the user's device.

## 5. Analytics and Advertising

QR Lens does not currently use:

- Analytics services
- Advertising services
- Tracking SDKs
- Marketing trackers
- Third-party telemetry services

QR Lens contains no advertisements.

## 6. Children's Privacy

QR Lens does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

QR Lens does not provide accounts, social features, communications, behavioural advertising, or user profiling.

## 7. External Links

QR Lens can open URLs contained within QR codes.

Opening a URL transfers the user from QR Lens to the headset's web browser. Websites opened through QR Lens are independent third parties and are responsible for their own privacy and data practices.

Users should review the privacy policies of websites they choose to visit.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if QR Lens gains new functionality or if its data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

If a future version of QR Lens introduces functionality that collects, stores, or transmits user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that functionality is released.
